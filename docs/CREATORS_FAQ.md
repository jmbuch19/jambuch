## About the studio

**What is Varta Vishva Studio?**
We turn your written stories into narrated audio stories and illustrated
videos, in Gujarati, Hindi or English. You write; we give your story a voice,
pictures, music and sound.

**Who is behind it?**
We are the team behind the YouTube channel વાર્તા વિશ્વ – Story Tube, where we
publish the complete Gujarati Ramayana and world classics. The same studio
and the same care go into your story.

**Who is it for?**
Writers, poets, teachers, families keeping memories, community groups — anyone
with a finished story they'd like people to hear and see. You must be 18 or
older to order.

**Is this the same as the free Courtyard?**
No. The Courtyard on jambuch.in is a free space for sharing writing. The studio
is a paid service that produces finished audio and video.

## What we make

**What can I order?**
- **Audio story** — narration, with or without music and sound effects.
- **Video with one picture** — your narration over a single illustration.
- **Video with moving paintings** — a series of illustrations with gentle
  camera movement, music, sound and captions.
- **Shorts pack** — 3 or 5 vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, Reels or WhatsApp
  status (with any video).

**What are the finish levels?**
Basic (સરળ), Standard (સુંદર) and Premium (શ્રેષ્ઠ). Each level sets how many
pictures, voices and extras you get. You can see the same short story at all
three levels on our page before choosing.

**What is "Studio-made" and what is "Varta Vishva Directors"?**
- **Studio-made:** our studio produces your story following your brief, and our
  team checks quality.
- **Varta Vishva Directors:** a director from our team works on your story
  personally — a 30-minute call, a mood plan for you to approve, voiced
  excerpts for you to approve, and the pictures shown to you before the final.
  It takes longer and costs more. Premium is always made by our directors.

**Can you make a fully animated film with moving characters?**
Not as a standard order. Our videos use illustrated paintings with camera
movement. For real animation, write to us for a separate quote.

**What can't you do?**
We tell you up front, so there are no surprises:
- lip-synced talking faces;
- an exact likeness of a real person;
- readable text drawn inside paintings (we add text separately, clearly);
- fast fight or action choreography;
- copyrighted characters (film, cartoon or brand characters);
- perfect pronunciation of rare dialect words without your help — so we ask
  you for a pronunciation list.

## How it works

**What are the steps?**
1. Tell us about your story — a few simple questions, about 10 minutes.
2. We prepare a one-page creative plan and a fixed price.
3. You approve the plan and pay for a sample.
4. You hear or watch your sample.
5. If you like it, pay 50% and we make the full piece.
6. You preview it and tell us about any errors; we fix them.
7. Pay the remaining 50% and download your files.

**Do I need an account?**
Yes, a simple one. You sign in with your email and a one-time code — no
password to remember. Your account shows every step of your order.

**What do I need to have ready?**
Your finished story as typed text (paste it, or upload a Word, text or PDF file
with real text, not a scanned image), its title, and the author's name.

**What will you ask me?**
What the piece is for and who will watch, how it should feel, the look and
colour you like (or "suggest for me"), your characters, music wishes, anything
to avoid, and an ending line or dedication. Most questions have a
"you decide for me" choice. You can type or record a voice note.

**Will you change my words?**
Only if you allow it. By default we narrate your text exactly as written. If you
let us shorten or adapt it for video, every change is shown to you in the
script, and nothing is narrated until you approve it.

**My story is long. Can you still make it?**
Yes. Stories over [20,000] characters are made as a series of episodes. We
suggest where to split; you decide.

## Price and payment

**How much does it cost?**
Orders start at [₹999]. Your exact price depends on length, format, finish level
and extras, and you see a **fixed quote before paying anything**. The quote
doesn't change unless you change your order.

**Why does it cost what it costs?**
Every order uses paid voice, illustration and AI services, plus our team's time
to check and finish your work. Our price covers those costs fairly — the quote
shows what's included.

**What is the sample, and what does it cost?**
A short piece of your own story, so you can hear or see our work before
committing. Studio-made sample: [₹499]. Varta Vishva Directors sample: [₹999].
**The sample fee is deducted from your full price** if you continue.

**How do I pay?**
In two parts after the sample: 50% to start full production, and 50% before you
download the final files. We accept UPI, cards and net banking through Razorpay.

**Is GST included?**
GST at 18% is added to the price as shown in your quote, and you receive a
proper GST invoice. **(check: show only once the GSTIN is active)**

**Are there hidden charges?**
No. Extras (more voices, Shorts, a second language, series storage) are chosen
by you and shown in the quote.

## Voices, languages and look

**Which languages?**
Gujarati, Hindi and English. You can add the same piece in a second language;
we translate, and you approve the translation before narration.

**Plain or dramatised narration?**
- **Plain:** clear, steady reading, like an audiobook.
- **Dramatised:** emotion, pauses, whispers, characters acting their lines. You
  approve the directed script first.

**Can characters have different voices?**
Yes. Depending on the level, your main characters can each have their own
voice; you choose from voice previews.

**Can my own voice narrate it?**
Yes, as an add-on. You upload a recording of your own voice and give consent.
We never copy anyone else's voice — no celebrities, no other people, no one
who has passed away.

**Will another writer get the same voice or music as me?**
Our voices and music come from a library, and we vary them between orders so
your piece feels your own. Library voices and music are not exclusive, though.
If you want a voice used only for you, choose the **exclusive narrator voice**
add-on.

**Which picture styles can I choose?**
Indian miniature, Pahari-inspired miniature, soft watercolour, storybook
illustration, ink and wash, realistic period film, and classic black-and-white
cinema style — each with examples. With colour treatments such as full colour,
sepia, black and white, muted, night tint, or colour slowly fading to black and
white. Not sure? Choose "suggest for me".

**Can I send my own photos as reference?**
Yes — your own photos or drawings only, for example your village or your
grandmother's photo for the general look. Not film stills or other artists'
work. We don't recreate a real person's exact face.

**Can you show gods and goddesses?**
Yes, respectfully. You decide whether a deity's face is shown or only symbols
(feet, hands, light, objects).

## Your rights and credits

**Who owns the finished work?**
You keep all rights to your story. You receive the finished files to publish
and use as described below. We never sell, license or alter your work.

**Where may I publish it?**
Online and offline: YouTube (including monetised channels), Spotify, Instagram,
Facebook, WhatsApp, your website, family and community events.
**Not included:** film festivals, TV broadcast, cinema or games. For those, we
need to replace the music first — ask for the music replacement add-on.

**Will I get copyright claims on YouTube?**
Our music and sounds are licensed for publishing on your own channels. If you
ever receive a claim on music we supplied, write to us and we'll help resolve
it.

**Can I order a story I translated or adapted?**
Yes, if you have permission from the rights holder, or if the original is in
the public domain (in India, generally when the author died more than 60 years
ago). You tell us where the story comes from, and the credits name the original
author and you.

**How are credits shown?**
Credits are generated from what you declare — author, translator, original
work — and locked, so they're always correct. Every video carries a short
note that voices and illustrations are AI-assisted.

**Is it really AI?**
Yes. The voices and pictures are created with AI tools; our team directs,
checks and finishes them. We say so openly in the credits, as the platforms
expect.

## Privacy and deletion

**What happens to my story after the work is done?**
We delete your story and all files from our storage **7 days after
delivery** — including your brief, drafts, scripts, previews and pictures — and
send you a deletion certificate. Download and keep your files safely before
then.

**Do the AI services keep my story?**
Your story is processed by AI service providers (Anthropic and ElevenLabs) to
make your order. They may keep processing records for a limited period (up to
30 days) under their own privacy policies. **(check: keep the training sentence
only if verified for both)**

**What do you keep after deletion?**
Only what we must: your account details, order history, invoices and payment
records (tax law requires us to keep these for several years). No creative
content.

**Do you reuse anything from my order?**
Only general sounds or music we created (such as rain or temple bells), which
may be reused in other works. Your story, narration, pictures and anything made
only for you are never reused.

**My story is a series. Do you delete each episode?**
For continuing stories you can choose **Series Storage** (paid), so we keep your
characters and style consistent across episodes. Your files are kept until the
date shown, with reminders before deletion.

**Who on your team sees my story?**
Only the people working on your order. Every team member signs a
confidentiality agreement, and access is logged.

## Publishing with us

**Will you publish my story on your channel?**
Only if you say so. Each order has one choice:
- **(A) Download only** — we publish nothing.
- **(B) Download and allow us to publish** — on Story Tube, jambuch.in and
  Spotify, credited to you.

**Do you earn money from my published work?**
No. If you choose (B), we switch off monetisation on your video. You can
withdraw permission at any time, and we take it down within 7 days.

## Corrections and refunds

**What if something is wrong in the result?**
Flag it on the preview — mark the moment and say what's wrong. We check it
against your approved plan and script:
- **Our mistake** (a wrong word, a mispronounced name you listed, a picture
  that contradicts the plan): fixed free.
- **A new wish** that wasn't in the plan: a content change. Small changes up
  to [20%] of the piece are included once; larger changes are quoted;
  changes over [50%] are a new order. **(check against brief 6E)**

**Can I get a refund?** **(check every line against brief section 8)**
- If we can't make your story at all (for example, it can't pass our content
  guidelines), nothing is charged.
- The sample fee pays for the sample once it's delivered.
- If a director hasn't started your order by the promised start date, you get a
  full refund.
- After you download the final files, the order is complete and can't be
  refunded.

## Content guidelines

**Is there anything you won't make?**
We don't make content that is hateful, sexual, promotes violence or harm, mocks
a community or faith, defames a real person, or copies someone else's
copyrighted work (including film song lyrics). The full list is on our
guidelines page.

**What if part of my story isn't allowed?**
We tell you plainly what the problem is and suggest a change — for example,
replacing quoted song lyrics with your own lines. You edit and we check again,
free.
