How a room is made

1

You send your work, however you have it

WhatsApp, email, a Drive folder, a notebook photographed page by page. No design skills needed.

2

I shape it into your room

Your name, your words about yourself, your colour. I show you a preview first.

3

Nothing goes public until you say yes

Later, you can add new work yourself from your phone — or keep sending it to me.

My promise to every guest

Your name is on every piece. Credit always goes to you, never to this site.

Your work stays yours. You choose how others may use it.

No ads, and no money is made from your work here.

Leave any time. Your room comes down and every file comes back to you.

Want a room, or know someone who deserves one?

Rooms are by invitation, for people I know. Tell me about them and I’ll get in touch.

Thank you — I’ll call or write soon.
