The Courtyard

Friends, family, colleagues — and you. A poem, a photograph, a lesson that helped a student, a small tool that saved someone an hour, a song an elder sang: if you made it, or are keeping it for your family, there’s a place for it here. Credit always goes to the person who made it.

In the old courtyard, nobody competes. Someone begins a story and the others listen. When it ends, someone else picks up the thread. Just closeness, laughter and sharing.

Your work deserves a better home than a WhatsApp forward.

Why share it here rather than anywhere else?

A home, not a feed

A post disappears in two days. Here your piece gets its own page with your name on it, still there years from now.

A real reader

I read every word you send and write back myself, whether or not it goes up.

Something to point to

For students and teachers: a published, credited link for your CV, portfolio or application.

Kept for the next generation

Letters, recipes, songs an elder sang: safe from a broken phone, credited to the person who made them.

What I won’t promise: big numbers. What I will: care, credit, and permanence.
